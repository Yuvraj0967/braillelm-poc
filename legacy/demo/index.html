What is happening inside

SmolLM2-135M · 30 layers · 49,329 tokens
STEP 01

Braille is tokenised as braille

The native arm adds 177 braille tokens: 27 base cells plus 150 merges learned with BPE. A 54-cell sentence costs 24 tokens here — 0.44 per cell — while the stock tokenizer spends about 3 per cell. The control arm keeps that stock vocabulary, which fragments each cell into several byte-level pieces.

STEP 02

New tokens start with meaning

Braille-aware fine-tuning initialises each new braille token with the average embedding of the print string its cells spell — the, ing, storm. The model does not have to learn what a cell looks like from zero.

STEP 03

A small adapter learns the mapping

LoRA r=8 on q_proj and v_proj, 460,800 trainable parameters, one epoch over 480 synthetic pairs. Decoding is greedy, 64 new tokens, on CPU. No retrieval and no reference is visible to the model.

Scope: the model was trained on a closed 138-word synthetic corpus with three sentence patterns and six-dot Grade 1 braille. It is a proof of concept for the tokenizer, not a general braille translator. Sentences outside that distribution are expected to fail.
